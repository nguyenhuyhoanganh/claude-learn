And let's say that this will have two variants inside it: Chicken and Steak. And I'll once again provide my Debug trait syntax above. So '#[derive(Debug)]'. And then I can choose to attach this enum as a piece of associated data for my 'RestaurantItem' variants. And let's say that it's only going to be stored for the Burrito and for the Bow.
