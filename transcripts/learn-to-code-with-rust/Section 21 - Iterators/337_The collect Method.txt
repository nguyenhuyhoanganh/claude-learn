And finally we can use a new method called 'collect' on the iterator. The 'collect' method exhausts the iterator and collects or in other words, gathers the resulting values from the iterator into a new collection type, such as a vector. So you can think of the 'collect' method as the equivalent of doing a for loop over the iterator, and pushing each yielded value into a brand new vector. It's just that the 'collect' method accomplishes the same result much more elegantly and declaratively, and thus it is generally preferred. It's just cleaner. So let me show you an example.
